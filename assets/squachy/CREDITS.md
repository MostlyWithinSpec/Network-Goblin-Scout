# Squachy

Squachy is the mascot of **SquachWatch-CYD** by skizzophrenic:
https://github.com/skizzophrenic/SquachWatch-CYD

These four frames are Squachy's waving cameo, cut pixel-for-pixel from the SquachWatch
README demo (`docs/look-up.gif`, the drone card), with the black background made transparent.
They are used **with the author's permission** (SquachWatch issue thread, October 2026: "please use a
small Squachy sprite, with credit and a link back to this repo"). Squachy is not covered by
this repository's licence; ask the SquachWatch author before reusing him elsewhere.

Scout shows him when a SquachWatch with SquachMesh transmit on is nearby (`social/SquachVisit.h`).
`tools/gen_assets.py` packs the frames into `src/ui/assets/SquachyArt.h`.
